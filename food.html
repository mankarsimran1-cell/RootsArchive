Local Food & Farming

Discover the agricultural traditions, local produce, and food culture of Palghar and Dahanu.

Dahanu: The Land of Chikoo

Dahanu in Maharashtra is well known for chikoo, also called sapota. Chikoo cultivation is an important part of the area's agricultural identity and supports the livelihoods of many local farming families.

The fruit is sweet and soft when ripe. It is enjoyed fresh and can also be used in milkshakes, desserts, and other food preparations.

Local Farming Traditions

Agriculture is closely connected with rural life in Palghar district. Farmers grow different crops and produce according to local conditions, available water, soil, and the season.

Traditional farming knowledge is passed from one generation to another and remains valuable to many rural communities.

Food and Community

Food traditions reflect the region's diverse communities and local ingredients. Families may prepare different dishes for everyday meals, festivals, and special gatherings.

Recording local recipes and food memories can help preserve cultural knowledge for future generations.

Why Farming Matters

Farming provides food, employment, and income for rural communities. Sustainable agricultural practices can also help protect soil, water, and the surrounding environment.

Did You Know?

Dahanu is widely associated with chikoo cultivation. The fruit is one of the best-known agricultural products linked with the area.
